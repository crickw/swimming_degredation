Time predictor — what would your time be at another age?

Enter your gender, event, current age, and a recent time. The model uses the same USMS age-grading ratios as the stroke comparison page (based on 2004 USMS Top-10 data) to project an equivalent time for every age from about 18 to your event's oldest supported age group, plus a few years of extrapolation on each end (shown dashed). This is a rough motivational estimate, not a scientific forecast — individual trajectories vary a lot based on training history, injuries, and technique.
